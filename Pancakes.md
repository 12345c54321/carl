# **Fluffy American Pancakes**
## **Prep time: 10 minutes**

Cook time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4 (makes about 8–10 pancakes)

Ingredients
1 ½ cups all-purpose flour

3 ½ tsp baking powder

1 tsp salt

1 tbsp white sugar

1 ¼ cups milk

1 large egg

3 tbsp melted butter (plus extra for the pan)

Optional: 1 tsp vanilla extract, maple syrup, and fresh berries for serving.

Instructions
Mix Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.

Combine Wet Ingredients: In a separate jug or bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter (and vanilla extract, if using).

Mix Batter: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Gently stir with a fork or whisk until just combined. Tip: Do not overmix; a few lumps in the batter are completely fine and actually help make the pancakes fluffier!

Heat the Pan: Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly coat it with a little butter or oil.

Cook the Pancakes: Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake onto the hot pan. Cook until bubbles start to appear and pop on the surface (about 2–3 minutes), then flip carefully.

Brown Both Sides: Cook for another 1–2 minutes on the other side until golden brown.

Serve: Serve warm stacked high with butter, maple syrup, and your favorite berries.
